# STARTER-KIT #5 — Faceless Playbook (base: capítulos 1–2 + calendário modelo)

## A. Capítulo 1 — The Faceless Model (rascunho pronto — expandir para 10p)

**The math (3 cenários honestos):**
- Conservative: $27 product × 37 sales = $999. At 1% conversion → need 3,700 qualified views (~120/day for 30 days).
- Base: $27 front + $9 bump (25% take) = $29.25 avg → 35 sales → $1,024.
- Ladder: 30× $27 + 10× $49 upsell + 5× $99 bundle (later) = $1,795 from 30 buyers.
**3 traffic lanes (pick 1 primary):** Short video (TikTok/Reels/Shorts — fastest 0→1k), Pinterest (slow, compounding, faceless-native), Reddit/X + SEO (best for Notion/Sheets niches).
**Rules:** 1 product, 1 lane, 30 days. No rebrand before day 30. Post > polish.

## B. Capítulo 2 — Pick Your Lane (quiz — colar no ebook)

Score 1–5 each: (a) I love systems/templates, (b) I love design, (c) I live in AI tools, (d) I love teaching/writing.
- Mostly A → Lane Templates (Ch.3A: #1/#3/#4 deste repo como referência de build)
- Mostly B → Lane Design kits (Canva/printables)
- Mostly C → Lane AI packs (prompts/workflows)
- Mostly D → Lane Playbooks (teach 1 skill in 30–80 pages)
Decision matrix table: build time / price / competition / repeat buyer. (Template lane = fastest cash; Playbook = highest authority.)

## D. Setup checklists (cap 3–4)

**Lemon (20min):** product + PDF/bump + test purchase + receipt e-mail check.
**Beacons (15min):** avatar (logo), name keyworded (`@faceless.systems | digital products`), 3 links (Free starter / $27 playbook / Bundle), auto-DM "starter".
**IG 9-grid starter:** 3 proof/value DEMOs, 3 B/As, 2 story-selling, 1 intro ("I sell X for Y without showing my face").
**Bio formula:** `I help [avatar] get [result] without [pain] | 👇 [freebie] + [product]`.

## E. Troubleshooting tree (cap 8 — 1 página visual)

- 0 views (<200/post after 10 posts?) → hooks weak OR no retention: fix = first 1s text + pattern (B/A wins) + post 2x/day 7 days + 20 comments/day in niche.
- Views but 0 profile clicks → bio/offer mismatch: fix = 1-line promise + 1 freebie link + pin best DEMO.
- Clicks but 0 sales → product/page gap: fix = demo video on page + 3 proof shots + FAQ + price $19–$39 + bump.
- Sales but < $1k → scale: affiliates 40% + bundle + repost winners + 2nd lane (Pinterest).
