# #8 — Remote Job Getter Kit (Resume + LinkedIn + Cover)

**1-liner PT:** Kit para conseguir remoto internacional: 8 CVs ATS + LinkedIn teardown + 50 covers + tracker.
**EN title:** *Remote Job Getter Kit — ATS Resumes + LinkedIn Makeover + 50 Cover Letters*

## 1. Validação

- **Categoria:** Resume templates + career guides — perenes no Etsy (milhares de vendas, $8–$29) + dor global "remote US job".
- **Avatar:** BR/LATAM/EU querendo remoto em USD, inglês intermediário, aplica e nunca ouve de volta. Quer CV que passa ATS + LinkedIn que atrai recruiter + cover rápida.
- **Transformação:** de "200 aplicações, 0 replies" → "CV ATS 8.0 + LinkedIn keyworded + 10 aplicações/dia em 30min + tracker + follow-ups".

## 2. Oferta

**Formato:** 8 resumes (Canva + Google Docs, ATS-safe + 2 designer), LinkedIn teardown guide 25p, 50 cover paragraphs bank, tracker sheet, vídeo 15min.

**Os 8 CVs:** SWE, Data, Design, Marketing, Support/CS, PM, Finance, Generalist — 1 página, US format (no photo, Month YYYY, action verbs, metrics), ATS fonts (Inter/Calibri), .docx + PDF export guide.
**LinkedIn guide:** headline formulas (3), about rewrite (5 blocos), experience bullets (STAR-lite), skills SEO (15 keywords por role), featured + open-to-work settings (recruiter-only), 1ª DM + connection note scripts.
**50 covers:** 10 openers × 5 bodies? Não — 50 blocos combináveis: 10 hooks + 20 proof bodies + 10 remote lines + 10 closers = "monta cover em 8min".
**Tracker:** 100-row applications (company, role, link, date, status, follow-up, salary, notes) + funnel (applied → screen → interview → offer) + follow-up dates.

**Plus (+$12):** interview pack (30 Q&A EN + salary script "USD range" + 5 questions to ask), portfolio 1-pager Canva, referral DM scripts.
