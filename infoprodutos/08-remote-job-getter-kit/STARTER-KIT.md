# STARTER-KIT #8 — Remote Job Getter (base: CV spec + LinkedIn + covers)

## A. CV spec (aplique nos 8)

- US 1-page: Name | City, Country (Remote EST-overlap) | e-mail | LinkedIn | GitHub/Portfolio | Phone optional. NO photo, NO birthdate, NO graphics rating bars.
- Sections: Summary (2 lines: role + years + stack + 1 metric) / Skills (ATS keywords line, mirror job post) / Experience (3–4 roles max, 3–5 bullets each: verb + task + metric + tool) / Education / Certs.
- Verbs bank: built, shipped, grew, cut, automated, led, launched, improved, owned, drove. Metrics: %, $, time, users, uptime, NPS.
- Fonts: Inter/Calibri 10.5–11pt, margins 0.6in, single column (ATS!), export PDF (text-selectable, test: Ctrl+A copy).
- Canva → Docs port: rebuild with Tables OFF (Docs tables confuse parsers — use tabs/line breaks). Teste: Jobscan free + copyslect.
- 1-pager per role com summary + skills exemplo preenchido (não lorem!).

## B. LinkedIn makeover (25p outline + formulas)

- Headline (3 fórmulas): `[Role] | [Stack x3] | [Metric] for [who]` / `I help [who] [outcome] | [proof]` / `[Role] open to remote | [timezone overlap] | [1 keyword string]`.
- About (5 blocos): hook (1 line) / what I do (3 bullets) / proof (2 metrics) / looking for (remote/contract, overlap) / CTA (DM open + e-mail).
- Experience: 3 bullets STAR-lite each + media link (project/doc).
- Skills SEO: pin top 3 (match target posts) + 12 more; endorsements ask script.
- Settings: Open to Work (recruiter-only, 5 titles, remote, locations US/EU), Creator mode? só se posta.
- Connection note (300 chars) + follow-up DM scripts (3): recruiter, hiring manager, referral ask.
- Before/after: 1 teardown real anonimizado com screenshots numerados (o vídeo segue este).

## C. 50 cover blocks (combináveis — 8min/letter)

- 10 Hooks: "Your post for [role] reads like my last 2 years: [X]…" / "I [metric] with [stack] — exactly your [requirement]…" etc.
- 20 Proof bodies: 1 STAR 3-line each (S+T / A / R metric) por skill comum (API, dashboard, support CSAT, campaign ROAS…).
- 10 Remote lines: "Based in [city], 4h overlap with EST, async-first (Notion/Slack), contractor via Deel/EOR OK, English C1 daily standups."
- 10 Closers: "Happy to do a paid trial task this week — 2 slots open." + "15-min intro? [Calendly]."
- Assembly: Hook + 2 bodies + remote + closer = 180–220 words. 3 full examples (junior/mid/senior) preenchidos.

## D. Tracker sheet (spec)

Colunas: Date | Company | Role | Link | Salary range | Source | Status (Applied/Screen/Interview/Offer/Rejected/Ghost) | Follow-up date | Contact | Notes | CV version (v1/v2).
- Conditional: Ghost >14d = laranja. Funnel: COUNTIFS by Status + chart + reply rate (=screens/applied).
- Follow-up scripts: D7 nudge + D14 breakup ("close the loop? happy to stay in touch").
- Ritual: 10/dia em 30min (tracker + timer) + weekly review (reply rate <5%? → rewrite summary/skills).

## E. Salary cheat (1-pager por role — 8 linhas)

Exemplo linha: `Support L2 remote US: $25–$45/h contractor | ask: "$35–$45/h depending on scope, open to trial"` + script: "My range for this scope is [X–Y]. Flexible for the right team — what's budgeted?" + levels.fyi/Glassdoor links.
